Receipt robot, 28 restaurantsn8n · Airtop browser · LLM extraction · JS · ran in production

Supplier receipts sat behind a member login with no API. Someone logged in for every restaurant and emailed each PDF to bookkeeping. Now an n8n workflow drives a real browser every second night at 02:00 and sends the three newest receipts per account.

  1. Δ1Clicks and browser prompts failed at night. Read the "Email as PDF" links and open them directly instead.
  2. Δ2The model wrapped its JSON in markdown. A Code node now strips it, parses it and splits one item per receipt.
  3. Δ3Runs "succeeded" with empty PDFs. Compared three URLs and found the date encoded twice:

P&L audit, 5 runs a monthn8n · QuickBooks API · monday.com · ran in production

Sales swing, and costs swing with them, so dollar amounts can't tell you if a P&L is right. The workflow measured every line as a % of total income and compared it with that line's share over the previous 12 months, using a deviation formula to flag what moved out of line.

General notes

  1. Reconstruction. The originals are owned by the company I previously worked at. I rebuilt them from my own notes with sample names and numbers. The logic and fixes are mine.
  2. Automate 80–90%, queue the rest. A short exception list a person clears in minutes beats a brittle "fully automatic" flow.
  3. Fix the data before the dashboard. Clean categories first, or reports spread the error faster.
  4. Debug with evidence. Compare what the system sent with a known good case, field by field.
  5. Write it down. A workflow isn't done until it has a note: what it does, when it runs, who to ask.

First 90 days at Kubermatic
